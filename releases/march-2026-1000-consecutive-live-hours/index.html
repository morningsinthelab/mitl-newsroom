Mornings in the Lab Surpasses 1,000 Consecutive Live Hours Without Missing a Single Day, Cementing Position as YouTube’s Longest-Running Independent Morning Show

TORONTO, ONTARIO, CANADA — March 2026 — Mornings in the Lab with Keith, Jon & Friends has surpassed a milestone that no other independent creator has reached in the morning daypart: 1,000 consecutive hours of live morning programming — without missing a single day. Now in its third season, the daily LIVE morning show streams every weekday at 8:00 AM ET on YouTube — building a loyal community of men 30–55 who start their day with accountability, fitness, mindset, and real conversation.

The milestone arrives at a moment when YouTube’s dominance over traditional media has never been more pronounced. MoffettNathanson recently crowned YouTube “the new king of all media,” valuing the platform at $500–$560 billion after it generated $40.4 billion in ad revenue for 2025 — more than Disney, NBCUniversal, Paramount Skydance, and Warner Bros. Discovery combined. According to Nielsen’s January 2026 Gauge report, YouTube commands 12.5% of all U.S. television viewing, more than any single streaming service or cable network. Yet the morning daypart — the hours between 6:00 AM and 10:00 AM — remains virtually unprogrammed by independent creators.

“We showed up at 8 AM when nobody else would — and we haven’t missed a single day. Over a thousand consecutive hours of live morning television, three seasons deep, building a community one conversation at a time — that’s not a content strategy, that’s a lifestyle.”

— Keith Bilous, Founder, MiTL Studio

The Morning Daypart: Streaming’s Biggest Untapped Opportunity

Nielsen data shows streaming now accounts for 47% of all U.S. television viewing, up 71% since 2021. Streaming even surpassed the combined share of broadcast and cable for the first time in May 2025. Despite this seismic shift, the morning hours remain dominated by legacy broadcast formats — Today, Good Morning America, CBS Mornings — that haven’t meaningfully evolved in decades.

Mornings in the Lab fills that gap with a format built for how men actually consume media in 2026: live on YouTube, available on-demand across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and all major platforms, with daily clips and shorts driving discovery. Now in Season 3 with over 1,000 consecutive live hours and a perfect attendance record, the show’s 5,700+ YouTube subscribers tune in not for polished production, but for raw, unscripted accountability — a format that traditional morning television has never attempted.

Expanding the MiTL Studio Universe

MiTL Studio, the live conversation and community studio behind the show, continues to scale its programming footprint. The MiTL Sports Desk (universe.mitl.studio) operates an AI-powered correspondent network covering all 32 NHL markets with dedicated local coverage — the first initiative of its kind in independent sports media. The studio’s digital ecosystem now spans over 30 connected properties, including the Look In My Mirror accountability mirror collection, the Inside the Lab newsletter, and multiple Substack publications targeting morning routine optimization and men’s personal development.

With 1,700+ videos on YouTube, over 1,000 consecutive live hours across three seasons without a single missed day, and a growing catalog of clips reaching new audiences daily, Mornings in the Lab is building the infrastructure for what independent morning programming looks like in the streaming era.
